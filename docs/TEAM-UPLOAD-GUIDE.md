# Member photo and signature guide

[Upload your photo, signature and details here](https://github.com/vkulesh-netizen/student-assignment-tracker/upload/main/docs/member-submissions).

[Read the full instructions](member-submissions/README.md).

Members first need to accept the repository invitation and sign in to the GitHub account that received it. The repository is private.

Use distinct filenames containing your name. Supply a current headshot, your student number, and your own signature after reading and agreeing to the contract. You can also download the presentation and contract, update only your own entries, and upload an individual version for the coordinator to combine.
