# Team photos and signatures

After accepting your repository invitation, sign in to the GitHub account that received it.

## Upload your files

[Open the upload page](https://github.com/vkulesh-netizen/student-assignment-tracker/upload/main/docs/member-submissions)

Choose **Add file > Upload files** if you are viewing this folder instead of the upload page. Select your files, enter a short commit message, and commit them. Each member should use their own name in filenames:

- `Hamzah-photo.jpg`, `Kevin-photo.jpg`, `Rihab-photo.jpg`, or `Ebana-photo.jpg`: a clear current headshot.
- `YourName-signature.png`: your own signature image, preferably on a transparent or white background.
- `YourName-details.txt`: your full name and student number, plus confirmation that you reviewed and agree to the contract before authorizing use of your signature.

Vitaliy's student number, portrait and signature are already included in the prepared documents.

The coordinator can insert each submitted photo and student number into the presentation and place each authorized signature on that person's contract line. Submit your own files using distinct names so nobody overwrites another person's documents. All repository collaborators can view these files in this private repository.

## Edit the documents yourself

1. Download the latest project ZIP or the individual files in `docs`.
2. In PowerPoint, open `Team Presentation Starter.pptx`. Add your student number next to your name on slide 1. On slide 2, use **Insert > Pictures > This Device** to insert your headshot above your name. Resize/crop the photo to fit your column, then remove your photo placeholder.
3. In Adobe Acrobat, open `Team Contract Partially Signed.pdf`. Read the contract. Use **Fill & Sign** to add your student number on page 1 and your signature and date on your own line on page 2. Sign only after you agree to the contract. Keep the existing signatures intact.
4. Save an individual version, such as `Team-Contract-Kevin-signed.pdf`, in this folder. Tell the coordinator which version you updated. Coordinate changes to the shared PowerPoint and contract one person at a time.

The contract still needs an agreed meeting time and any remaining team decisions. A signature image is not proof that all members agreed or signed.
